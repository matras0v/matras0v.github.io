What exists

Six parts,
one build.

This is not a website with a product somewhere behind it. Every piece below was designed, written and deployed in the same pass, which is also why any of it can be changed in an afternoon.

01

The bot

The entire product surface. Sign-up, trial, payment, keys and device management all happen in one Telegram thread — @GrimeVPNbot.

02

The backend

Issues and revokes the keys behind each subscription, and keeps a person's five device slots in sync with what they have actually paid for.

03

Billing

Seven days free before anything is charged, then 119 ₽ a month or 999 ₽ a year. The same numbers in the bot and on the site.

04

Devices

Up to five per person, unlimited traffic, one node in Finland. Stated plainly because those are the numbers a buyer is actually comparing.

05

Name, wordmark, voice and colour. Deep indigo and one signal colour, because a privacy product should not look like a casino.

06

The site

One page that runs the bot's own onboarding inside itself, so a visitor has used the product before deciding whether to install anything.

How it was built

Three decisions

Everything else on the site and in the bot follows from these.

Decision 01

No screenshots — the product explains itself

A VPN bot is hard to show in a picture: the actual product is a conversation. So the homepage doesn't describe the bot — it runs the bot's own onboarding, live, in the page.

The site is not a picture of the product. It is the first ninety seconds of using it.

Decision 02

Real numbers, not marketing language

Five devices. One node, in Finland. Unlimited traffic. A seven-day free trial before anything is charged, then 119 ₽ for a month or 999 ₽ for a year — worked out per month, that's about 83 ₽. The pricing on the site is the pricing in the bot, exactly.

Decision 03

One person, the whole stack

The product, the bot, the backend issuing and revoking keys, the billing, the brand and the site are the same build. Nothing here is outsourced or bolted on — which is also why it can be fixed in minutes when something breaks.

The front door

Running, right here

The same onboarding a new subscriber sees, live below rather than recorded. The chat in the page is the real script; the last message opens the real bot.
