The brief

An official distributor,
not a marketplace

ShineMate is a polishing-equipment manufacturer; this is the site of its official distributor in Russia. That single fact decides most of the design: a buyer arriving here is choosing equipment for professional work, and the page has to read like a specialist who stocks the range rather than a storefront that resells it.

What the work was

Five decisions

Decision 01

A product-first homepage

The first screen shows the equipment. Not a company statement, not a slider of stock photography — the machines themselves, at the size they deserve, with the one sentence that says what they are for.

Someone who already knows what a rotary polisher is should be able to tell within a second that they are in the right place.

Decision 02

Catalog by product family

Machines, pads, compounds and accessories are separated clearly enough that a professional who knows what they need can go straight to it, without reading past three categories that do not apply to them.

Decision 03

Compatibility is the real product

The useful part of a catalog like this is not the individual items — it is knowing what works with what. A machine takes a particular backing plate; that plate takes particular pads; those pads pair with particular compounds.

Getting one link in that chain wrong means the parts do not fit, so the site treats the chain as the thing being sold rather than as a specification buried in a table.

Decision 04

Product pages that explain the purchase

Each product answers the same four questions in the same order: what it is, what it is for, the specifications that matter, and what it works with. A buyer comparing two machines is comparing the same fields in the same places.

Decision 05

Russian, because the market is Russian

The site is written for buyers in Russia and stays in Russian throughout, including for wholesale enquiries. This portfolio switches language; the client's site does not, because it is not a portfolio piece — it is a working sales channel for one market.
