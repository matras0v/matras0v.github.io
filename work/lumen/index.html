The clinic

One room, one injector, one hour.

Lumen is not a chain and does not run a laser on a conveyor. You are booked into one room, with one clinician, for the whole appointment — the same person who assessed your face is the person holding the needle.

Every injectable is placed by a nurse practitioner. Every laser is run by a licensed laser technician who works only on our devices. Nobody at Lumen is paid a commission on what you buy.

  • We will tell you when a treatment will not fix what you came in for.
  • No packages, no memberships, no pressure to prepay for a course.
  • A two-week review after every first treatment, included.

Questions people actually ask

The awkward ones first.

Will I look like I had something done?

That is a dosing decision, not a treatment decision. The look people are afraid of comes from too much product placed in too few visits, usually because someone wanted a dramatic result on the first appointment.

We under-dose the first session on purpose and review you at two weeks. If it needs more, more is free within that window. It is much easier to add than to wait out.

How long before I can go back to work?

Every treatment on this site publishes its downtime, and the planner above gives you the number for your specific plan. Injectables are usually same-day. Resurfacing lasers are the ones that need a week, and we will say so before you book rather than after.

What happens if I do not like it?

Neuromodulators wear off in three to four months, so nothing is permanent. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved in a fifteen-minute appointment, which we do at no charge if we placed it.

Results from lasers and microneedling are gradual by design, which is why we photograph you at every visit under the same light.

Why are your prices ranges instead of numbers?

Because injectables are priced by how much product your face needs, and that is not knowable from a website. A first-time forehead is usually the bottom of the range. A strong frown that has never been treated is usually the top.

You get a single fixed number in writing at the consultation, and it does not change on the day.

Do I have to buy a package?

No. We do not sell them. Treatments that genuinely need a course — lasers, microneedling, hair removal — are priced per session so you can stop whenever you want to.
