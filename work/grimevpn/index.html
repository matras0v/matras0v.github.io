Fair questions.

Why only one country?

Because a long list of countries is mostly marketing. Every node costs money to keep fast, and a service this size does one route well rather than forty badly.

Finland is the choice because it is close to most of the people using this, which is what actually determines whether video loads.

What happens after the free week?

The keys stop working and the bot asks whether you want to carry on. Nothing charges you quietly, because there is no card on file to charge.

Five devices — do they all work at once?

Yes. Each one holds its own key, so a phone, a laptop and a TV box can all be connected at the same time without knocking each other off.

Is this a big company?

No. It is one person who built the bot, the backend and the billing, and who answers the messages. That is the honest answer, and it is also why the price is what it is.
