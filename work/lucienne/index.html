How it is made

Two factories.
Both named.

Outerwear and tailoring are cut in Porto by a family workshop we have used since the first season. Knitwear comes from a mill outside Barcelos that spins its own yarn. We publish both names on the care label, which is unusual and should not be.

Producing fourteen pieces instead of ninety is the whole business model. It is why there is no sale, no outlet, and no second-quality channel — there is no surplus to move.
