Why MotoRefine

Two brothers, one detailing van, and a standard that didn't change when the climate did.

The workshop started on the streets of Chicago — mobile, small, and obsessive about finish. Dubai asked harder questions of paint: relentless UV, fine sand, heat that punishes a clear coat within a season. The answers got better. The standard stayed the same.

Certified, not self-declared

STEK certification and Gtechniq approval are granted after training and audit. Both are on the wall, and both are on every car that leaves.

Built for this climate

Film and coating choices are made for Dubai heat and sand, not copied from a European spec sheet.

Inspection before quotation

Every car is looked at under studio light before a price is given. Paint condition decides the work, not a package name.

Questions

Before you message us.

How do I get a quote?

Message the studio on WhatsApp with the car, the year and what you're asking about. We look at the paint in person before we give you a number — that's step two of the three above.

Why does the car need an inspection first?

Paint condition decides the work, not a package name. Two cars of the same model can need completely different prep, so we quote after we've actually looked at yours under studio light.

PPF or ceramic coating — which do I need?

PPF is a physical film that absorbs stone chips and scuffs. Ceramic coating is a bonded layer that repels water and dirt and adds gloss — it doesn't stop a chip. Most cars we protect long-term get both: film where it takes impact, ceramic over the rest. Ask us which panels need which.

Can I just message you on WhatsApp?

Yes — it's how most of the studio's work starts. Message us directly and we'll take it from there.
