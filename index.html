How it works

No discovery phase, no deck, no retainer. You tell me about the business, I build the site.

01

You talk, I listen

One call or one long message. What you sell, what you charge, who keeps asking you the same question on the phone. That question usually becomes the homepage.

02

You see it in two days

A real site at a real address, not a picture of one. You open it on your phone, send it to your partner, and tell me what's wrong with it. I fix it the same day.

03

It goes on your domain

Under your accounts, with your passwords, which I never need. Then I hand you a plain-English page on how to change your own prices.

You own it.
All of it.

  • The files live on your computer. Hand them to any developer and they'll understand them in a minute.
  • Hosting is free at your size, on your own account. No monthly software, no licence, nothing to cancel.
  • Your domain stays yours. I never hold it, and I never need your passwords.
  • No page builder underneath. Nothing that can be taken away or start charging you later.

About

I'm Vadim Ivanchenko. I work alone, which is the point: you talk to the person building the thing, and nothing gets lost between a strategist, a designer and a developer who have never met your customers.

Most of my clients run a business where the phone ringing is the whole job. A detailing bay. A polishing shop. A clinic. They don't need a brand system. They need a page that answers the three questions every caller asks, before the call.

I write every site by hand, in plain HTML and CSS. That's not nostalgia — it's why the pages load instantly on a phone in a parking lot, why there's no monthly bill, and why you can walk away from me at any time and take the whole thing with you.

I'm based in Russia and work remotely with clients in the US and the UAE — in English, or in Russian if you prefer.
