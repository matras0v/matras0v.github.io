Inside.

Interiors are delivered finished: stone floors, full-height glazing, and joinery fitted rather than delivered flat-packed.

A quiet building on a loud stretch of water.

Aurelis is one slab, oriented so that every residence faces the channel and no residence looks into another. The podium holds the arrival court, the pool deck and the residents' floor; everything above it is private.

The facade is precast stone with recessed glazing, which is unfashionable here and deliberate: it keeps the afternoon sun off the glass, so the rooms behind it stay usable at four in the afternoon in July.
